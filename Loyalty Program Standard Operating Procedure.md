# **Standard Operating Procedure: Loyalty Program Workflow**

This document provides a detailed, narrative, and instructional guide for the overall Loyalty Program workflow and the Monthly Update process. It outlines the responsibilities of the Human Capital People Management Analyst (HC PM), Team Leaders/Leads, HC Admin & Analyst, and the Partners (Mitra).

## **Part 1: Overall Loyalty Program Workflow**

This section narrates the end-to-end journey of the Loyalty Program, from token accumulation to the final distribution of rewards.

### **Phase 1: Token Accumulation and Data Cut-Off**

The process begins with the **Partners (Mitra)** entering the "Earning Day" period. During this phase, Partners accumulate tokens based on the total number of sprints and slots they have taken.

**Instruction for HC PM:** \* **Execute the Data Cut-Off:** You must strictly observe the cut-off dates for token accumulation. For Period 1, the accumulation runs from December 16th to June 15th, with the cut-off on June 15th. For Period 2, the accumulation runs from June 16th to December 15th, with the cut-off on December 15th.

### **Phase 2: Data Processing and Notification**

Once the cut-off date is reached, the **HC PM** takes over to consolidate the data. The HC PM processes various datasets, including Exclusive Partner Data, Slot Data, Sprint Data, Project Rejection Data, and the Techno Employee Database.

This data is then integrated into the Loyalty Program Dashboard. For the Operation and Telephony Center, the dashboard tracks Exclusive Status, Cumulative Slots, Regular Slots, Downgrade/Reset checks, Total Tokens, Grading, and Membership Type. For the Techno Center, it tracks Total Sprints, Sprints per Period, Total Tokens, Project Rejections, Downgrade/Reset checks, Level, and Partner Status.

**Instructions for HC PM and Team Leaders:**

* **Distribute Token Information (HC PM):** You must extract the total token data and send it to the respective Team Leaders/Leads and relevant Heads. This information must be distributed via Email in PDF format, categorized by function.  
* **Notify the Partners (Team Leader/Lead):** Upon receiving the token reports, you must immediately inform your respective Partners about their total accumulated tokens, the number of tokens eligible for redemption, and the token expiration details.

### **Phase 3: Redemption Process and Verification**

Following the notification, the **Partners** proceed to fill out the Loyalty Program Redeem Form. This form contains the complete catalog of items available for redemption.

After submissions are closed, the **HC PM** extracts and curates the data from the Redeem Forms. This is a critical curation step to ensure all conditions are met.

**Instructions for HC PM:**

* **Verify Redemption Eligibility:** You must verify three main criteria:  
  1. Ensure the Partner has a sufficient token balance for the selected catalog item.  
  2. Confirm that the Partner holds an "Active" status on the designated redemption day.  
  3. Verify that the requested item matches the allowed category for that Partner.  
* **Confirm Partner Status:** You must cross-check and confirm the employment status of the redeeming Partners with their Team Leaders/Leads using an Excel format.  
* **Handle Resignations/Ineligibility:** If a Partner is found to have resigned or does not meet the requirements, update their data status to "Redemption Failed" (Gugur Redemption).  
* **Inform Ineligible Partners (Team Leader/Lead):** You must communicate with the ineligible Partner and provide a clear understanding of why they cannot proceed with the redemption.  
* **Approve Eligible Partners:** If the Partner meets all criteria and is actively employed, update their data status to "Successfully Redeemed" (Berhasil Redeem).

### **Phase 4: Procurement and Distribution**

Once the final list of successful redeemers is confirmed, the **HC PM** informs everyone of the final Loyalty Program Redeem Status. Following this, the HC PM proceeds to purchase the requested goods.

**Instructions for HC PM and Partners:**

* **Schedule Pickup (HC PM):** After the items are purchased and ready, you must announce the official schedule for item collection.  
* **Execute Handover (HC PM):** Facilitate the item pickup process accurately.  
* **Prepare Documents (Partners):** You must bring mandatory physical documents to the pickup location: your Partner ID Card, KTP (National ID), and NPWP (Tax ID). If you are authorizing someone else to pick up the item on your behalf, a formal Power of Attorney (Surat Kuasa) must also be provided.

## **Part 2: Monthly Update Loyalty Program Workflow**

This section describes the routine monthly maintenance required to keep the Loyalty Program dashboard accurate and up to date.

### **Phase 1: Data Request and Acquisition**

The monthly cycle initiates with the **HC PM** reaching out to the **HC Admin & Analyst** to request the latest operational data.

**Instructions for HC PM and HC Admin:**

* **Request Data (HC PM):** You must initiate the data request to the HC Admin & Analyst via WhatsApp.  
* **Provide Data (HC Admin & Analyst):** You must generate and hand over the requested data. For the Operation and Telephony Center, provide the Exclusive Partner Data and Slot Data. For the Techno Center, provide the Sprint Data and the Techno Employee Database.

### **Phase 2: Database Update and Reporting**

Armed with the fresh monthly data, the **HC PM** proceeds to update the core systems. The first step is incorporating any new Partners who have joined during the month.

**Instructions for HC PM:**

* **Register New Partners:** You must add the names of new partners into the system every month, utilizing the latest active partner data.  
* **Update the Dashboard:** Input all the newly acquired data (Exclusive Partner Data, Slot Data, Sprint Data, and Techno Employee Database) into the central Loyalty Program Dashboard to ensure real-time accuracy.  
* **Generate Monthly Report:** Finally, utilize the updated dashboard to create the official Human Capital (HC) Monthly Report.
